"Let urgent alerts through" (on by default) keeps 🚨 alerts and loud My alerts audible. The header shows 🌙 while it's quiet.
- **12- or 24-hour clock** (in the same card): the phone's setting, 24-hour or 12-hour, for every time the app shows: alerts, the calendar, the widget and quiet hours.
