- **Calendar: Landing and lift-off rules** (about 6 December), a major entry with a countdown and reminders. From then, a ship lands on a world or lifts off from it on its own drive only when its lift beats the world's gravity, at every surface place including our own outposts, tugs and freighters too. A fleet lands only if its weakest ship can; one that can't holds orbit over the place and still defends it. The entry lists the gravity of the worlds where we have places (Ganymede 1.43 m/s², and so on). The date is fixed in the game's own rules rather than the API, so only a game update can move it.
