- **My alerts** (Settings › My alerts): your own thresholds, on top of the bridge's alerts.
  - Kinds: money below or above an amount; income falling below a level (0 = turns negative); a good at one of our places running out within N hours; crew supplies at a place running out within N hours; current research finishing within N minutes; a good's stock on one of Earth's markets falling below a share of its target; a spaceport's wait falling below N days.
  - Places, goods, markets and spaceports are picked from lists of your own, filled from the checks' reads.
  - Each alert shows its current value ("Now $612M, −$8M a day"), can be switched off without deleting it, and is quiet or loud.
  - It fires once when its line is crossed, and again only after the value has gone back past it (also across restarts).
  - Checked once a minute from replies the checks already read, so no extra data, except a spaceport wait: one small read every 10 minutes while such an alert is on (and one when the bridge starts, for the list of spaceports).
  - They appear under Company · My alerts on the Alerts tab.
