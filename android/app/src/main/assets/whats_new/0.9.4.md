- **New from the bridge.** When a bridge update adds alerts the app doesn't know yet, you can try them straight away:
  - Their on/off switches appear in Settings › Checks under "New from the bridge", with the bridge's own description (first one: rail link posts from bridge 1.10.00).
  - Their alerts show under their tab's "New" filter on the Alerts tab (rail links under Company › New), and on the Alert types page marked "new", with loud, quiet or off as usual.
  - A later app update then gives them a proper place.
- **What's new.** After each app update, this list of changes pops up once. Settings › Status › App updates has a "What's new" button to see it again.
